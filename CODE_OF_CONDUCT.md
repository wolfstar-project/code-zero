# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Code Zero community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, appearance, ability, experience, education, nationality, race, religion, sex characteristics, gender identity, or sexual orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for different viewpoints, constructive feedback, responsibility for mistakes, and a focus on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insults, sexualized conduct, personal or political attacks, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement

Community leaders may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate these standards. Report incidents privately to the WolfStar maintainers through [join.wolfstar.rocks](https://join.wolfstar.rocks). Maintainers will protect the privacy and security of reporters as far as reasonably possible.
