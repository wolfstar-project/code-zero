## What this site collects

The marketing site you are reading is fully prerendered and holds no session, no account, and no
database — visiting it does not create a record anywhere in Code Zero's infrastructure beyond
whatever your hosting provider's own access logs capture.

## What the hosted service collects

The Team and Enterprise tiers process the source code and metadata of the repositories you connect,
solely to run the review and fix workflow you configure. Model calls are made to the provider you
select in repository policy, under that provider's own data-handling terms.

## Data you provide directly

Contact-form and support communication (see [Contact](/contact)) is used only to respond to you.

## Your choices

You can request a copy or deletion of any data the hosted service holds about your account by
opening a support request through the channels on the [Contact](/contact) page.
